Our Story

Cocina peruana casera

Sazón Criollo cooks the coastal-creole way — bright ceviche cured to order, pollada nights that bring people together, and the lentejas and salsa criolla that show up at every family table.

Nothing here is made ahead of time. Every order is cooked fresh, the way it’s supposed to be.
